A little reassurance

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Every cat has their own little ways. Share yours with the team before you book.

Does the website confirm my hotel slot?

No. This preview prepares an enquiry for WhatsApp. You still need to press Send in WhatsApp, and the hotel must confirm availability, price, check-in arrangements and any deposit. No room is held by this website.

What should I prepare before a stay?

Ask the hotel about vaccination records, current health, food, medication, familiar items and check-in requirements. Share your cat's routine and any handling concerns so the team can confirm what it can accommodate.

Can I add grooming to my cat's stay?

The enquiry form lets you request a grooming session alongside the sample hotel option. The business will need to confirm that the requested service is offered and suitable for your cat, and agree a time and final price.

How do the returning-customer discounts work?

The 5% returning and 10% regular tiers are a proposal for this prototype, not existing verified offers. The demo applies one discount to accommodation only. The hotel must approve the programme and verify past completed stays before granting any reward.

Is this the hotel's official booking website?

This is an independent client-presentation prototype, not an owner-approved official booking service. Publicly listed contact details are used, while room categories, prices, grooming packages and rewards are demonstration proposals. Photos are clearly identified by source.

Keeping things clear
Privacy & preview notes.
This is an independent website concept for presenting to Cyberjaya Loving Pets Grocery & Hotel, not an owner-approved official website.
Enquiries, not reservations
Booking and grooming forms prepare WhatsApp messages. They do not check real-time capacity, hold slots, receive payments or confirm services. You need to press Send in WhatsApp and receive confirmation from the business.
Sample prices and rewards
Stay names, sample capacities, nightly rates, grooming menu prices, and 5% / 10% rewards are demonstration proposals. They must not be treated as existing hotel offers. Customer tier is self-declared and must be verified by the hotel. Grooming is excluded from loyalty discounts.
Your information
This page does not use analytics, trackers, advertising, account login or persistent browser storage. Personal form entries remain in page memory until the page is closed or refreshed. Clicking a WhatsApp button puts the details you entered into a WhatsApp URL and draft; they may also be retained by your browser history and WhatsApp. External sites and the hosting provider have their own privacy practices.
External connections
Default preview images and all application code are embedded in this HTML. Google Maps is loaded only when you choose Load Google map. Facebook, Google, WhatsApp and the directory source open when you click their links. No automatic message is sent.
Photos and reviews
Business photos and Google/Facebook customer-review text were not accessible during preparation. The original profiles are linked instead. No individual testimonial is invented. Any later imported review is labelled as supplied by the site editor, not automatically verified.
Public information sources
The phone and address were cross-checked in PetShopNearMe and Cybo. The displayed rating snapshot comes from Infobel. Checked on 22 October 2026. Hours differ across sources, so no fixed hours are promised.
Before official launch
Obtain the owner's approval, accurate service and pricing details, reuse permission for every photo and review, a confirmed WhatsApp number, and appropriate production privacy and booking terms. This prototype is deliberately marked noindex.
